<objective>
Make the watchlist clickable and give the centre column a real chart. Selecting a ticker is a
synchronous client-state change that swaps the main chart panel to that ticker's accumulated
price series — the same buffer the sparklines already draw from.

Purpose: UI-03 is the phase's drill-down gesture — the thing that turns ten small lines into
one readable chart. It is also where the centre column stops being a single bordered box
around the positions table and becomes the stacked-panel layout the heatmap and P&L chart
plug into next.

Output: `chartSelection.tsx` + its provider in the layout, `MainChart.tsx`, a clickable and
keyboard-reachable `WatchlistRow`, and a restructured centre column in `page.tsx`.
</objective>
